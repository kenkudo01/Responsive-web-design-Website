About Me

CS Student & Systems-Oriented Software Engineer

Who I Am

I am a Computer Science student at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE), focusing on systems and software engineering. I am particularly interested in how design decisions affect system reliability, performance, and security.

My Skills

My work spans compiler construction, concurrent and distributed systems, and architecture-level refactoring. I also develop practical software extensions, including Revit API plugins, using languages such as C++, Java, Python, and C#.

My Interests

I am deeply interested in understanding how systems fail, how vulnerabilities emerge from design choices, and how secure, maintainable systems can be built. Currently, I am expanding my knowledge in security engineering, cloud infrastructure, and SRE practices.

History

My academic journey and development as a software engineer

  • 2022

    University Enrollment

    I enrolled in Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) to study Computer Science. Through my studies, I built a strong foundation in programming, algorithms, and system design.

  • 2023 -

    Software Development & Engineering Studies

    While studying at ELTE, I worked on various programming projects, including object-oriented design, data structures, and application development using Java and Python.

  • 2024 -

    Add-in Development for Architectural Software

    I started working on the development of software extensions for architectural design tools. Through this work, I gained hands-on experience in designing, implementing, and maintaining practical engineering solutions.

  • Present

    Ongoing Growth as an Engineer

    Currently, I continue my studies at ELTE while improving my skills as a software engineer. I am especially interested in building tools that support real-world workflows and users.

Favorites

A glimpse into my interests and hobbies beyond coding

Activities

Outside of my academic work, I enjoy exploring new technologies through personal projects and experiments. I often use side projects as a way to deepen my understanding of software design and to try ideas that are not limited by coursework.

Food

Cooking is one of my favorite ways to relax and reset my mind. I especially enjoy simple homemade dishes and experimenting with new recipes, treating cooking as a creative process similar to programming.

Sports

I value maintaining a healthy balance between mental and physical activity. Sports help me refresh my mindset and improve focus, which positively impacts my productivity in development work.

Other Interests

I am interested in understanding how technology supports real-world workflows and everyday life. This curiosity drives me to learn not only how systems work internally, but also how users interact with them.
